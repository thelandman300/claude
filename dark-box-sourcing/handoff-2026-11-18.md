## How skip tracing works

Skip tracing turns a name and mailing address into a cell phone and email.

1. The county record gives an LLC as the owner. The Secretary of State filing names the person who runs that LLC.
2. Upload that person's name and address to the skip trace tool.
3. It returns phones and emails, ranked by how likely they are to be current.

What to expect:
- About 60 to 80% of names come back with a working phone, and fewer with an email.
- It works on people, not companies, so the Secretary of State step comes first.
- Check numbers against the Do Not Call list before calling.
